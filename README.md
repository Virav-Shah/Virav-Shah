## Featured work

### 🔭 [Quro — NASA Bioscience Research Platform](https://github.com/Virav-Shah/Quro)
A full-stack research assistant for **500+ NASA bioscience papers**, combining cited RAG answers with an interactive knowledge graph.

`React` `FastAPI` `Qdrant` `Neo4j` `Gemini`

### 🎙️ [Agentic AI Photo Editor](https://github.com/Virav-Shah/Agentic-AI-Photo-Editor)
A local voice-and-text interface that converts natural-language requests into multi-step edits across **nine image tools**. Built for the Adobe product-development track at Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.

`Ollama` `Llama 3.2` `Gemma 3` `JavaScript`

### 🧬 Multimodal Medical Image Registration
A deep learning system for 3D brain MRI registration using a differentiable deformation field, achieving **0.768 Dice overlap** and a reported **100–150× speedup** over classical solvers.

`PyTorch` `3D U-Net` `GPU kernels` `OASIS MRI`

### 📉 [NSE Stock Risk Forecasting](https://github.com/Virav-Shah/VaR-CVaR-Calculator-for-NSE-Stocks)
Explainable quantile forecasting for **95% and 99% risk**, with portfolio analytics and SHAP-based explanations across 10 NSE stocks.

`LightGBM` `SHAP` `VaR/CVaR` `Streamlit`
